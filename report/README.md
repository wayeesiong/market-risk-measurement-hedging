# Report

This folder contains the final academic report for the market risk measurement and hedging project.

The report evaluates the market risk of a $1 billion Hong Kong equity index portfolio and compares several risk measurement and hedging approaches, including:

- Portfolio beta analysis
- Historical Simulation VaR
- Delta-Normal VaR
- Monte Carlo VaR
- Expected Shortfall
- E-mini S&P 500 futures hedging
- E-mini S&P 500 put-option hedging
- Hedging outcome comparison
- Silo and firmwide risk management considerations
